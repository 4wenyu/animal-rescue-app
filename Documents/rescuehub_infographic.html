⚖️ Pillar 1: Intake & Public/County Handoffs (摄入与交接)

Regulatory Baseline: General public surrenders require an immediate "Transfer of Ownership Waiver" executing legal forfeiture. Stray animals cannot be immediately adopted; they are subject to statutory "Stray Holds" (typically 3-7 days) mandated by county or state laws. Platforms must hold state-level Department of Agriculture PACFA (or equivalent) licenses to broker adoptions.

监管基准： 公众放弃宠物需要立即签署“所有权转让豁免书”。流浪动物不能立即被收养；必须遵守县或州法律规定的法定“流浪动物保留期”（通常为3-7天）。平台必须持有州级农业部PACFA（或类似）许可证才能代理收养。

⚠️ Legal Warning (法律警告): Prematurely clearing an animal for adoption before the local stray hold expires exposes the 501(c)(3) to theft of property lawsuits from the original owner.

💰 Pillar 2: Crowdfunding & Solicitation (众筹与广播)

Under the Charleston Principles, operating a digital portal that actively targets donors in a specific state triggers mandatory Charity Solicitation Registration in that state.

根据查尔斯顿原则，运营一个积极针对特定州捐赠者的数字门户网站，将触发该州强制性的慈善募捐注册。

🏢 Pillar 3: Sponsorship Loops & UBIT (赞助与UBIT税务)

IRS regulations separate tax-exempt Qualified Corporate Sponsorship Payments from taxable Advertising Income (Unrelated Business Income Tax - UBIT). Perks from vendors (Chewy, Purina) must remain neutral.

美国国税局将免税的合格企业赞助付款与应纳税的广告收入区分开来。来自供应商的福利必须保持中立。

Safe / 免税 (Sponsorship):
  • Vendor Logos & Slogans (供应商标志)
  • Value-neutral descriptions (中立描述)
  • "Sponsored by Petco" (由Petco赞助)
Risk / 应税 (Advertising):
  • Qualitative/comparative language (比较性语言)
  • Price details or specific calls to action (价格或购买呼吁)
  • "Buy Purina for the best nutrition"

📱 Pillar 4: App Strategy & Integration Schema (应用策略与架构)

A cohesive data strategy links legal intake, medical hold status, and gamified retention. The database must isolate an animal's legal availability from its fundraising visibility. 连贯的数据策略将合法接收、医疗保留状态和游戏化留存联系起来。数据库必须将动物的合法可用性与其筹款可见性隔离开来。

User Retention Loop Strategy (用户留存循环策略)

To maximize MAU (Monthly Active Users), the app utilizes push notifications tied to animal rehabilitation milestones, paired with UBIT-compliant sponsor rewards.

  1. Fund/Share: User donates to a stray's medical wallet. (用户捐款)
  2. Milestone Update: Push notification sent when animal clears "Stray Hold" and finishes surgery. (接收保留期结束通知)
  3. Adoption Alert: User notified when the animal is adopted. (收养提醒)
  4. Vendor Reward: User receives a neutral "Thank You" 15% Chewy coupon for participating in the animal's journey. (获得供应商奖励券)
